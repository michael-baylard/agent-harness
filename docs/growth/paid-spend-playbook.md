# Harness + CI paid spend playbook

> _Last updated: 2026-09-09_ · Budget: **$2–4k/mo** · Assumes zero CI clients at start.

## Rule

**Harness gets distribution. CIaaS gets dollars.** Do not run paid ads to the $49 one-time Harness SKU.

## Ranked allocation ($3k/mo example)

| # | Channel | $/mo | Why |
|---|---------|-----:|-----|
| 1 | Outbound infra (domains, inboxes, Instantly, enrichment) | 900 | One $2,997/mo CI close pays 12 months of spend |
| 2 | Google Search — bottom-funnel CI terms + competitor brands | 1,000 | Active buying intent at $3k–9k ACV |
| 3 | Content/SEO (VA + Ahrefs/Serper) | 400 | Feed `/harness/platforms` programmatic pages |
| 4 | LinkedIn retargeting (site visitors only) | 400 | Cheap frequency on warm CI/MCP traffic |
| 5 | Dev newsletter test (TLDR AI / Console) | 300 | Harness top-of-funnel; kill if CPA > $30/sale |

## Do not spend on

- Broad brand ads
- Full-time SDR at $4k with no pipeline
- Fake GitHub traffic
- Neon/Voyage embed of vendor doc corpora (Context7 covers live docs)

## Engineering ROI (ship order)

1. Cursor marketplace submit (free distribution)
2. `/harness/platforms` hub + 17 platform skill pages (SSG)
3. Wave 1 OG image (done in klarix)
4. Harness Pro $12/mo — **needs user approval** (keep $49 lifetime anchor)
5. Auditable policy layer — enterprise team SKU wedge
